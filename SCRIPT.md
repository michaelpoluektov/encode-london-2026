Hello, we are Shadily, this is our presentation for London AI Hackathon 2026.

We are running for the agents and creative tracks.

Our tools allows game developers and graphics designers to co-design graphics shaders with an AI agent.

Unlike existing tools, Shadily integrates the AI with a node editing setup, and allows the agent to preview shader renders natively in intermediate steps to debug complex rendering pipelines.

Designing shaders is a very technical endeavour, traditionally requiring a good understanding of GPU programming patterns, which creative professionals often lack.

Our tool aims to reduce the barrier of entry and make it more accessible.

Similarly, a one shot tool often doesn't give creative professionals enough flexibility to tweaks their designs and inject their own creative vision.

The tool is stable for regular use, and supports live editing, shader previews with various presets.
